## 3. Print & Formatting Specifications (PDF & Guides)

- **Print Resolution**: 300 DPI recommended for raster figures (Sentinel-2 false-color composites, inundation maps).
- **Typography Guidelines**:
  - Title: 72–84 pt (readable from 3–5 meters)
  - Section Headings: 36–44 pt
  - Body Text: 20–24 pt (minimum 18 pt)
  - Captions & References: 14–16 pt
- **Color Palette**: High-contrast, accessibility-compliant palette suitable for wetland and hydrologic visualization (blues for water, greens/browns for vegetation/soil).
