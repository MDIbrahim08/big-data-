# LAKE 2026 POSTER — VISUAL HOMAGE & LOGO INTEGRATION REPORT

**Concept**: *"The Living Wetland — Big Data Meets Ecological Conservation"*  
**Visual Homage Inspired By**:
1. **"Rivers of Water" Reference**: Vertical high-contrast waterbody flow, deep azure-to-teal gradient, overhead canoe observation motif, and bold atmospheric typography.
2. **"Kerala — God's Own Country" Reference**: Organic, curved shoreline boundary separating dense botanical tree canopies (`#065f46`) from aquatic water spreads, balanced with airy white editorial space.

---

## 1. Logo & Institutional Branding Integration
* **Chanakya University**: Prominently featured in the top-left header with verified SVG crest badges and academic affiliation ribbons (`School of Engineering, Chanakya University, Bengaluru`).
* **IISc Bengaluru & EWRG**: Positioned in the top-right header with institutional insignia acknowledging the *Energy and Wetlands Research Group (CES, IISc Bengaluru)*.
* **LAKE 2026 Symposium Branding**: Golden title sub-banner (`LAKE 2026: 15th BIENNIAL LAKE SYMPOSIUM`) with thematic callout: *Ecosystem Monitoring via Big Data, Remote Sensing, AI & Nature-Based Technologies*.

## 2. Creative Elements Implemented

| Creative Feature | Reference Inspiration | Scientific Implementation in Poster |
|---|---|---|
| **Hero Water Split** | Kerala / Rivers of Water vertical riverway split | Dynamic overhead vector waterbody with smooth gradient (`#0284c7` &rarr; `#0e7490` &rarr; `#064e3b`), depicting the Ankasamudra basin and its $>900\text{ m}$ separation from the Tungabhadra backwaters. |
| **Lush Forest Fringe** | Tree canopy borders | Curving deep emerald and moss green tree canopy zones representing the dense *Acacia nilotica* (Karijali) groves. |
| **Observation Vessel** | Traditional canoe in water stream | Gold-trimmed observation vessel marker on the baseline, serving as a symbolic motif for field and satellite calibration. |
| **Earthen Tank Bund** | Structural engineering arc | Heavy amber/gold arc (`#f59e0b`) tracing the physical southern/southeastern earthen bund and overflow spillway. |
| **Grid Architecture** | 3-column academic atlas layout | Balanced, card-based layout with zero blank space, visual process pipelines, formula badges, and 4 high-contrast QR codes. |
